# Product and MVP scope

## Product promise

Show users what they deposited, which separately funded proceeds they can allocate, where those proceeds moved, and what evidence has been submitted. Keep custody and transfer status visible.

## Features

- Wallet connection, network checks and transaction receipts.
- USDC deposit and withdrawal of available principal.
- Separate principal, sponsor-funded proceeds, allocated funds and transferred donations.
- Exactly two pool choices, pool details, allocation confirmation and receipt history.
- Dashboard, searchable activity, and fund-flow ledger with provenance.
- Evidence submissions, reviewer decisions and a public status history.
- Account preferences and admin controls with audit records.

## MVP

Support one explicitly configured network, its approved USDC contract, one wallet session, and manual allocation of eligible sponsor proceeds. The example routes all 0.20 USDC to Hunger Relief. Offer allocation to one pool per action; do not add a split-allocation editor. Multiple actions may allocate remaining proceeds to either pool.

Include the complete user journey and a minimal operations dashboard, recipient configuration, funded sponsor lot, evidence review queue, ledger reconciliation and paused-state handling. Use an explicit demo banner and synthetic receipts when no live transactions exist.

## Deferred

Automated investment strategies, actual yield accrual, APY displays, recurring deposits, fiat payments, cross-chain transfers, social feeds, additional pools, token incentives, and formal waqf products. Do not add insurance, charity accreditation, legal waqf status or guaranteed recovery claims.

## Success criteria

Users can identify the source of 0.20 USDC, distinguish it from their 3 USDC principal, trace an allocation to a transfer record, and see whether evidence is submitted, reviewed, or disputed. Financial totals reconcile independently of evidence status.
